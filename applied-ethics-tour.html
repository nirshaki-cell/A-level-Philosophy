Applied ethics

Three normative theories meet four practical issues: stealing, simulated killing, eating animals and telling lies. Applying a theory is the best test of whether you understand it, and the best way to find its weaknesses. The tour ends by applying meta-ethics to the same issues, as the specification requires.

Built from the departmental handouts Stealing, Simulated killing, Eating animals and Telling lies and the Applied Ethics Revision Tables, which are based on Lacewing, M. (2017) Philosophy for AS and A Level: Epistemology and Moral Philosophy (Routledge), ch. 3. The handouts have been reworded throughout. Material marked ‘Added’ or ‘Beyond the spec’ was not in those sources and should be checked. AQA 7172 · Moral Philosophy.
