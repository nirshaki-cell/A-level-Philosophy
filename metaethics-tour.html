Meta-ethics

When you say “torture is wrong”, are you stating a fact, expressing a feeling or giving a command? This tour covers moral realism (naturalism, non-naturalism, Moore and Hume) and moral anti-realism (Mackie’s error theory, Ayer’s emotivism and Hare’s prescriptivism), and ends with the issues that anti-realism faces.
