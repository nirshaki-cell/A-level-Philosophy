Substance dualism

Is the mind a thing in its own right, distinct from the body? Take the tour: Descartes’ indivisibility and conceivability arguments, the responses to each, and the issues facing dualism – other minds, Ryle’s category mistake and the interaction problem.

Text adapted from the departmental handouts Substance Dualism, Substance dualism and the problem of other minds and Conceivability and possibility, checked against the AQA 7172 specification and set texts (Descartes, Meditations VI; Ryle, The Concept of Mind; the Elisabeth–Descartes correspondence; Avramides, Other Minds). Images from the departmental slides. Boxes marked ‘Beyond the spec’ are extension only.
