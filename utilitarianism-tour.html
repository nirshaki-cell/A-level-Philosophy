Utilitarianism

The right action is the one that produces the most good. This tour covers what “utility” means and how to maximise it: Bentham’s calculus, Mill’s higher pleasures and his ‘proof’, preference utilitarianism, and act versus rule utilitarianism. It then covers the five issues on the specification.

Built from the departmental slides Utilitarianism (2024) and Sinnott-Armstrong, ‘Consequentialism’, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (Winter 2023). Material marked ‘Added to complete the spec’ was not in those sources and should be checked against the set texts. Material marked ‘Beyond the spec’ is extension only. AQA 7172 · Moral Philosophy.
