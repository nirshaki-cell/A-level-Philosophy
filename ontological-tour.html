Ontological arguments

Can we prove that God exists just by thinking about what God is? Take the tour: Anselm and Gaunilo, Descartes and Leibniz, the empiricist and Kantian objections, and the modal arguments of Malcolm and Plantinga.

Built from the departmental resources: The Ontological Argument slides, Kant and the ontological argument, the two Malcolm’s ontological argument decks (after Lacewing 2017, ch. 2), and Oppy, Rasmussen and Schmid, ‘Ontological Arguments’, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (Fall 2025). Material marked ‘Beyond the spec’ is extension only. AQA 7172 · Metaphysics of God.
