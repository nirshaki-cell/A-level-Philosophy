The problem of evil

If God is all-powerful, all-knowing and perfectly good, why is there evil? Take the tour: the logical and evidential problems, the free will theodicy, Plantinga’s free will defence, Hick’s soul-making theodicy and Midgley on human wickedness and free will.

Built from the departmental handouts The logical problem of evil and free will, The evidential problem of evil and Midgley on Evil and Free Will (after Lacewing 2017), and Tooley, ‘The Problem of Evil’, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. Material marked ‘Beyond the spec’ is extension only. AQA 7172 · Metaphysics of God.
