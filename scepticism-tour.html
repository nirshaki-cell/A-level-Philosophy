The limits of knowledge: scepticism

Could you be dreaming right now? Could everything you see be an illusion? Take the tour: what philosophical scepticism is, how far it reaches, the radical sceptical argument, and why philosophers take a view almost no one believes so seriously.

Based on the departmental lecture notes on scepticism and Comesaña & Klein, ‘Skepticism’, Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. AQA 7172 · Epistemology.
